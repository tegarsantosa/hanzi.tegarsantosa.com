This site is a free small place for everybody who wants to learn Hanzi.

I am writing and releasing this website at the Main Library of National Taiwan University. It came from my concern where it is quite difficult to find a simple, free, and effective tool to practice writing Chinese characters online, especially for me, during my internship research at NTU.

Wrapping my personal struggles in learning Chinese, I made this webiste to help myself and hopefully others who are learning Chinese characters. This tools will always be free and open source. If you have any suggestions, please feel free to reach out to me at iam@tegarsantosa.com.

Chinese is my 3rd language, and I know it is not easy to learn especially using my 2nd language to learn it. So let's learn together and have fun! 加油!
